Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

1. Overview

Welcome to Kluzar Tech ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. Our primary goal is to provide high-performance, secure tools for developers and security researchers. This policy explains how we handle data when you use our website (tech.kluzar.com).

2. How Our Tools Process Data

  • Client-Side Tools: JWT, JSON, regex, hashing, password, and similar browser tools process input locally using JavaScript and Web Crypto APIs. Those inputs are not submitted to our application server.
  • Network Inspection Tools: Target URLs are processed by a security-filtered network service for authorized public-site inspection. Private targets, credential-bearing URLs, and unsupported ports are rejected, and request output is not intentionally stored.

3. Data We Collect via Third Parties

To keep our tools free and monitor performance, we use industry-standard third-party services that may collect non-personally identifiable information (Non-PII):

  • Google Analytics (GA4): Collects anonymized usage data such as page views, browser type, and general location (country/city level) to help us improve user experience.
  • Google AdSense: Uses cookies to serve relevant advertisements based on your prior visits to our website or other websites on the internet.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

We use local storage strictly for functional purposes, such as saving your UI preference (Light/Dark mode) and PWA (Progressive Web App) caching. Google and its partners may use cookies for ad personalization. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

5. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Since we do not require user accounts or collect personally identifiable information (PII), we do not have a database of user profiles to delete or export. However, you have the right to disable cookies in your browser settings or use ad-blockers, and our core client-side tools will continue to function perfectly.
